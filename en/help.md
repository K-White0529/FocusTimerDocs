# Focus Timer Help

Focus Timer is a focus timer app that helps you put your phone down and stay focused.

## Basic Flow

1. Start a focus session from the timer screen.
2. Keep your phone placed down while focusing.
3. When the focus session ends, the app moves to a break.
4. When the break ends, start the next focus session.
5. After completing 4 sets, the app moves to a long break.

## Stamina

Starting a focus session consumes stamina. If you do not have enough stamina, you cannot start a focus session.

## Recovery Tickets

If your focus session is interrupted because you pick up your phone or leave the screen, you can use 1 recovery ticket to resume from the remaining time.

When you resume with a recovery ticket, the interruption is not finalized as a failure. Your consecutive success count is also kept.

Recovery tickets are granted at the following times:

- 1 ticket on first launch
- 1 ticket after 7 consecutive successful days
- 1 ticket after completing 4 consecutive cycles without a failure

The 7-day streak bonus is granted every 7 days, such as on day 7, 14, and 21. The 4-cycle streak bonus is also granted every 4 cycles, such as on cycle 4, 8, and 12.

If multiple conditions are met at the same time, you receive tickets for each condition.

## Notifications

The app can show local notifications when a break or long break finishes. On Android 13 and later, notification permission is required to show notifications.

## Failure Detection

During a focus session, picking up your phone, tilting it significantly, or leaving the screen may be treated as an interruption.

## Logs and Share Images

The log screen lets you review your focus records. You can generate a share image from your logs and share it through Android's share feature.
